Policyholder service guide

What Do You Need Help With?

Call us for help with the items below. Report new claims directly to your insurer.

Renewal review

Contact the office as soon as a renewal or nonrenewal notice arrives. Have the notice ready for a review and do not wait until the expiration date.

Billing or cancellation concern

Contact the carrier using the number on your bill or policy and call Office #3. Do not assume coverage is active, reinstated, or changed without written confirmation.

Claim guidance

For immediate danger call 911. Report a claim through the carrier's official channel and preserve the claim number. Office #3 can help explain the next steps but does not decide coverage.

Certificate or proof request

Have the written contract or requester instructions ready. A certificate generally provides evidence of coverage and cannot rewrite the underlying policy.

Policy change

Driver, vehicle, address, property, occupancy, payroll, operations, beneficiary, or contact changes may affect coverage. Request review before assuming a change applies.

Annual review

Review your policies at least once a year and after major life or business events. The review may confirm that no change is needed.

Documents

Use only the carrier portal or another specifically approved secure method for declarations, identification, claims, financial, medical, or underwriting documents.

Contact the office

Call 305-910-8850 for policy service. For urgent billing or claim deadlines, contact the insurer directly using the number on your policy.

FAQ

Questions about your existing policy

What should I do if I receive a cancellation or nonrenewal notice?

Read the reason, deadline and effective date right away. Contact the insurer using the number on the notice, then call Office #3 with the notice ready. Do not assume autopay, a late payment or a phone conversation has kept the policy active.

Call 305-910-8850 Renewal and billing checklist

How do I report an insurance claim?

Report the loss through the official claims number or portal listed on your policy. Keep your claim number and the adjuster’s contact details. Office #3 can help you find the right contact, but the insurer investigates the claim and decides coverage. Call 911 for immediate danger.

How do I add a car, driver or address change to my policy?

Call before the change takes place whenever possible. Tell us what is changing and the date you need it to apply. A request alone does not update your coverage; obtain written confirmation of the change, effective date and any premium adjustment.

Call 305-910-8850

How can I get proof of insurance or a certificate?

Check your insurer’s portal for available policy documents or call Office #3. For a business certificate, have the requester’s written requirements and deadline ready. A certificate must reflect the coverage actually in force.

Business certificate requests

Can I send policy documents through this website?

This website does not accept policy numbers, payments, claim files or document uploads. Call for instructions on an approved secure way to share documents, or use your insurer’s official portal. Do not send sensitive details in a public message.

Can I get policy service in Spanish?

Yes. Call 305-910-8850 and ask for English or Spanish service. Have your question and any notice ready so we can discuss the issue clearly.
