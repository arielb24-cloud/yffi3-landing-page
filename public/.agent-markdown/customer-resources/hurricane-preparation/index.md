Hurricane Guide

# Hurricane and Post-Storm Insurance Preparation

Save your insurance documents, review wind and flood coverage, and know how to contact your insurer before a hurricane. Follow local emergency instructions and put your safety first.

Customer checklist

## Your Checklist

Use these steps to prepare your questions and documents. Your policy and insurer determine what is covered.

### Before hurricane season

Save carrier claim and billing contacts, review declarations and deductibles, confirm how flood and wind are addressed, and keep documents in a secure location available away from the property.

### Create an inventory

Photograph rooms and higher-value property, keep receipts when practical, and store a copy securely outside the home.

### Protect people first

Follow local emergency-management and evacuation instructions. Never remain in danger to document property or prevent damage.

### After damage

Contact the carrier promptly through its official claims channel, record the claim number, photograph damage when safe, and take reasonable steps to prevent additional damage.

### Preserve evidence

Keep receipts for emergency measures and temporary expenses that may be relevant. Follow carrier instructions before discarding damaged items except when safety authorities require removal.

### Fraud awareness

Verify licenses, read contracts, avoid blank spaces, retain copies, and be cautious with pressure to sign or assign rights immediately after a storm.

## Hurricane insurance questions

Does hurricane coverage also cover flooding?

Wind damage and flooding are different insurance risks. Standard homeowners insurance generally excludes rising-water flooding, even when a hurricane causes it. Review your home policy and any separate flood policy before storm season, including coverage for the building and belongings.

[Review home and flood questions](/home-insurance/)

How much would I pay before hurricane insurance responds?

Check the hurricane deductible on your declarations page. If it is a percentage, it is generally based on the insured dwelling limit rather than the claim amount. Review the dollar amount, coverage limits and exclusions so you understand the potential expense.

[Florida hurricane deductible rules](https://www.myfloridacfo.com/division/consumers/consumerprotections/floridashurricanedeductible)

What insurance documents should I save before a hurricane?

Save your policy declarations, claim and billing contacts, a home inventory and photos of important belongings somewhere you can reach if you leave home. Keep receipts when available. Store copies securely; this website does not accept inventories or claim documents.

Can I wait until a storm warning to buy flood insurance?

Do not wait. Some flood policies have waiting periods, and insurers may restrict new coverage as a storm approaches. Ask about the actual effective date and any waiting period before relying on a new policy.

What should I do first after hurricane damage?

Put safety first and follow emergency instructions. When it is safe, document the damage and report it through your insurer’s official claims channel. Keep the claim number and receipts, and ask the insurer about its documentation and repair instructions before making commitments.
