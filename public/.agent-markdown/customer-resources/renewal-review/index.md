Renewal Guide

# Renewal and Cancellation-Prevention Review

Review your renewal notice as soon as it arrives. Check the new premium, coverage and deductibles, and tell us about any changes before your policy expires.

Customer checklist

## Your Checklist

Use these steps to prepare your questions and documents. Your policy and insurer determine what is covered.

### Read every notice

Confirm the carrier, policy term, due date, premium, installment plan, requested documents, reason for any nonrenewal or cancellation, and the contact method shown on the notice.

### Verify billing

Confirm the amount, due date, payment method, returned-payment history, and whether autopay information is current directly with the carrier or authorized billing party.

### Review changes

Identify new drivers or vehicles, address or occupancy changes, roof or property updates, new equipment, payroll or operations changes, beneficiary changes, and contact updates.

### Ask before expiration

Contact the carrier and Office #3 promptly. Some underwriting items, inspections, signatures, or replacement options require time.

### Require written confirmation

Do not assume a payment, request, quote, or conversation renews, reinstates, changes, or binds coverage. Keep written confirmation and receipts.

### Document the outcome

Record the date, channel, representative, confirmation number, effective date, required follow-up, and where the written policy documents are stored securely.

## Renewal, payment and cancellation questions

Why did my insurance premium go up at renewal?

A renewal price can change because of insurer rates, claims, coverage changes, updated property or driver information, or discounts that no longer apply. Ask what changed on your policy. Compare equivalent coverage and the full policy-term cost before deciding whether to switch.

[Call 305-910-8850](tel:13059108850)

What should I compare in my renewal quote?

Compare limits, deductibles, exclusions, covered drivers or property, payment terms and total premium for the same period. Check whether the quote includes the coverage your lender, landlord or contracts require. A lower payment can come with less protection or different fees.

Does a late payment automatically restore a cancelled policy?

No. Ask the insurer whether reinstatement is available and whether there is any gap in coverage. Get the status and effective date in writing. A payment receipt alone is not proof that coverage has been restored.

Should I act on a billing notice if I have autopay?

Yes. Check the notice and payment status with the insurer. Expired cards, returned payments or account changes can interrupt autopay. Confirm what is due and by when rather than assuming an automatic payment was successful.

When should I cancel my old policy if I am switching insurers?

Confirm the replacement coverage, effective date and time, required payment and written policy documents first. Then ask how to cancel the old policy and whether fees or refund rules apply. Coordinate the dates to avoid an unintended gap.
