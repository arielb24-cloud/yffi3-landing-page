# YFFI3 Bilingual Editorial Roadmap

**DRAFT — DO NOT PUBLISH WITHOUT SEARCH CONSOLE EVIDENCE, AUTHORITATIVE FACT CHECKING, AND INSURANCE/COMPLIANCE REVIEW.** No Search Console account or query export was available during this audit. The sequence below reflects the requested topic set and local customer journey, not proven demand. Reorder it after comparing the last 28 days with the previous 28 days by query, page, device, and language.

## Prioritization method

1. Prioritize real queries ranking in positions 4–20 or pages with high impressions and weak CTR.
2. Confirm the searcher's insurance question can be answered accurately without carrier-specific promises.
3. Use a Florida regulatory, statutory, federal, or other carrier-neutral primary source.
4. Have an authorized insurance reviewer approve English facts and a fluent reviewer approve natural Spanish.
5. Publish one useful canonical bilingual pair with reciprocal hreflang; do not create thin city variants.
6. Measure qualified calls, texts, quote starts, assisted quotes, and sold policies—not raw page views alone.

## Content template for each pair

- Plain-language answer in the first paragraph with a visible reviewed date.
- Local relevance limited to verified Miami/Florida context; no repetitive keyword blocks.
- A scope box: general information, not legal/tax/claims advice; coverage depends on written policy and underwriting.
- Source links to current primary materials.
- Questions to gather before calling, excluding sensitive information from the public form.
- Internal links to the relevant service page, quote page, privacy page, and the reciprocal language version.
- Genuine author/reviewer name and credential only after owner confirmation.
- Visible FAQ only when it answers real questions; add `FAQPage` schema only when the same Q&A is visible.
- One non-promissory CTA for phone, text, or secure quote help.

## Search Console feedback loop

For every published pair, record the publication date, query cluster, impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, engaged sessions, `phone_click`, `sms_click`, `quote_start`, qualified leads, completed quotes, and bound policies at 7, 28, and 90 days. Improve title/description only when the page accurately satisfies the query. Consolidate cannibalizing pages instead of creating more variants.

## Content explicitly excluded

- City doorway pages or swapped-place-name copies.
- Invented savings, price, approval, carrier, rating, review, award, or license claims.
- Carrier-specific advice without appointment and compliance approval.
- Automated Spanish publication without fluent editorial review.
- Advice based only on AI summaries or SEO blogs when primary sources exist.
- Review excerpts without source, permission, and faithful wording.
