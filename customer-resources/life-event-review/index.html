Annual Review

Annual and Life-Event Coverage Review

A new home, child, job or business can change what you need from your insurance. Review your policies once a year and after major changes.

Customer checklist

Your Checklist

Use these steps to prepare your questions and documents. Your policy and insurer determine what is covered.

Household changes

Marriage, divorce, birth, adoption, death, a new household member, a new driver, or a dependent becoming independent are reasons to review your policies.

Vehicle and travel changes

A new car, loan or lease, commute, garaging location, teen driver, rideshare use, recreational vehicle, boat, or reduced driving may matter.

Home and property changes

A purchase, sale, move, renovation, roof or system update, new valuables, rental use, vacancy, home business, or mortgage change may matter.

Business changes

New operations, locations, contracts, employees, payroll, revenue, vehicles, equipment, cyber exposure, or certificate requirements can affect the insurance your business needs.

Life insurance review

Review beneficiaries and whether needs changed after birth, adoption, marriage, job change, death, divorce, mortgage changes, or other major events.

Document the decision

Record questions, options discussed, decisions, required underwriting or payment steps, and the written confirmation of any completed change.

FAQ

When to review your insurance

When should I review my insurance policies?

Review at renewal and after a move, marriage, new child, new driver, major purchase or business change. A policy that fit last year may need updating. Make a list of what changed and the questions you want answered.

Does a move or renovation affect home and renters insurance?

It can. Tell the insurer about a new address, major renovation, rental use, vacancy or a home business before assuming your policy still fits. Updated belongings, building details or occupancy may change the coverage needed.

When should I review life insurance beneficiaries?

Review them after marriage, divorce, a birth, adoption or a death, and periodically with your overall policy review. Confirm changes through the insurer’s process. Ask for guidance before naming a minor or making a decision with legal or estate implications.

Life insurance questions

Do new employees or new services affect business insurance?

They can change your risks and policy requirements. Review new employees, payroll, locations, vehicles, equipment and services with the office. Do this before taking on work that your current policy may not cover.

Discuss business insurance

Will an insurance review automatically change my policy?

No. A review helps you identify whether changes are needed; keeping the same coverage may be appropriate. If you request a change, confirm approval, any payment and the effective date in writing before relying on it.
